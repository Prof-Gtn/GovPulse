MINISTRY: Ministry of Home Affairs, Immigration, Safety and Security
DATE: 2025-06-22
CONTEXT: Response to a crime wave in Windhoek

STATEMENT:
The Ministry of Home Affairs, Immigration, Safety and Security wishes to reassure the public that the recent increase in reported crime is being addressed through targeted operations. The Namibian Police Force has deployed additional resources to affected areas and is working closely with community policing forums. The Ministry calls on all residents to remain vigilant and to cooperate with law enforcement. Public safety remains the Ministry's highest priority.
