MINISTRY: Ministry of Agriculture, Fisheries, Water and Land Reform
DATE: 2025-04-18
CONTEXT: Response to FMD outbreak affecting northern farmers

STATEMENT:
The Ministry of Agriculture, Fisheries, Water and Land Reform wishes to inform the public that the recent outbreak of Foot and Mouth Disease in the northern communal areas is being contained through coordinated veterinary interventions. Movement restrictions have been imposed in affected areas and vaccination campaigns are ongoing. The Ministry is working with farming associations and international partners to ensure that affected farmers receive the necessary support. The Ministry calls on all farmers to comply with movement controls to prevent further spread.
