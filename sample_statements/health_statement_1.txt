MINISTRY: Ministry of Health and Social Services
DATE: 2025-08-10
CONTEXT: Response to reports of medicine shortages at public hospitals

STATEMENT:
The Ministry of Health and Social Services notes with concern the reports regarding the availability of essential medicines at certain public health facilities. The Ministry wishes to clarify that the reported shortage is due to a temporary delay in the national medical supply chain, which has now been resolved. Deliveries to affected facilities are underway and are expected to be completed within the week. The Ministry remains committed to ensuring uninterrupted access to essential medicines for all Namibians.
