MINISTRY: Ministry of Home Affairs, Immigration, Safety and Security
DATE: 2025-03-15
CONTEXT: Response to media reports about police conduct during a protest

STATEMENT:
The Ministry of Home Affairs, Immigration, Safety and Security notes the media reports regarding the conduct of officers during recent public gatherings. The Ministry wishes to clarify that the Namibian Police Force operates within the framework of the Namibian Constitution and the Police Act. Any allegations of misconduct are taken seriously and will be investigated through the established internal complaints mechanisms. Members of the public are encouraged to report any concerns to the nearest police station or to the Office of the Inspector-General.
