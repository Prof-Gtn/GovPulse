How it works

From newsprint to a ministry reply, in four steps.

Every story follows the same path, so nothing gets missed and nothing goes out unreviewed.

  1. 1

    Collect

    Print, radio and online news gathered from every source on a fixed schedule.

  2. 2

    Analyse

    Each story gets a sentiment rating, a short summary, and a list of officials named.

  3. 3

    Match

    The story is linked to the right Office, Ministry or Agency using 250+ entities.

  4. 4

    Respond

    The Response Desk drafts a reply from precedent. Ministry staff review and approve it.
